No finance background needed. ASX is the Australian Securities Exchange. IOZ is BlackRock’s exchange-traded fund (ETF): a tradable share basket tracking the S&P/ASX 200 large-company benchmark. A holding is one position in that basket; these 200 holdings are not the entire ASX.
01

Question 1 / Breadth or weight?

More names do not necessarily mean more weight.

Market capitalisation is share price multiplied by shares outstanding. Portfolio weight is a holding’s percentage of a fund’s stock basket. A sector groups similar businesses. Start with the full ASX directory; then compare counts and weights within the same 200 IOZ holdings.

02

Question 2 / Where does the imbalance sit?

Look inside the portfolio—and inside its sectors.

Concentration means much of the weight rests on few names. Test the whole portfolio, typical holdings, size bands and each sector’s leading three.

03

Question 3 / Has this balance always held?

The industry mix is a moving picture.

The Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) supplies historical market shares. Its three categories differ from the fund’s sectors. Compare the consistent series from 1980 to 2019, separately from the 2026 snapshot.

04

Question 4 / Is the economic setting equally resource-heavy?

Mining matters very differently across Australia.

Mining helps explain the economic settings of listed resource businesses. The Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) includes listed and unlisted activity. GVA, or gross value added, measures economic production—not ASX market weight or company performance.

05

Question 5 / What does a listing actually locate?

Registration and operations are different footprints.

Portfolio weight shows influence in the fund, not where a business operates. Two separate examples reveal different footprints: legal registration in ASX’s foreign-entity report, then BHP’s three Copper SA mines, located by Geoscience Australia (GA) and identified by BHP.

More names do not automatically mean more weight. Financials has 29 of the same 200 holdings and 33.3% of weight; Materials has 52 and 25.1%. Unequal position sizes produce that gap.

Ten holdings carry almost half the basket; history shows today’s mix is only a snapshot. Neither counts nor weights explain what those businesses actually do.

New Zealand’s 53 registrations show a legal footprint; BHP’s three mines show an operating footprint. These separate examples explain why a listing alone cannot locate a business. Registration is not headquarters, and the mines are not all BHP operations. Regional mining GVA is not ASX market capitalisation. IOZ describes a 200-stock fund, not the entire ASX.

Populations & calculations

ASX entries count once, retaining “Not Applic” and “Class Pend”. Directory industry groups and IOZ sectors differ. The 21 October directory supplies company identities and names; both count and weight shares use the same 20 October IOZ holdings. This does not compare performance or changes in membership between those dates.

Stock weight = holding value ÷ total stock value × 100. Cash and futures are excluded; equity market values are renormalised to 100%. The source’s rounded equity-row weights sum to 99.78% before renormalisation. This is not company market capitalisation.

Boxplots: boxes contain the middle half; the median is the middle value, averaging the middle pair when needed. Whiskers extend within 1.5 box lengths; separate dots are outliers. Logarithmic axes show multiplicative differences.

Geography & time

ABS 2024–25: current-price mining GVA ÷ total industry GVA, not GSP. This is whole-economy context, not company revenue. Australian maps use conic equal-area projections; the world map uses Equal Earth, with Mercator enlargements.

GA 2025 supplies actual mine coordinates; BHP’s July 2026 disclosure identifies the three Copper SA sites. The supporting state table counts 346 mines, excluding one Christmas Island record. Sites are not companies or headquarters.

ASX September 2026 report: 136 security codes / 135 entities / 13 jurisdictions. News Corporation’s two classes count once. Report coverage and exclusions appear under the map. Incorporation is not operating geography.

The latest ASX / IOZ downloads were checked on 21 October 2026: the directory is dated 21 October and holdings 20 October. These are fixed snapshots, without automatic updates; the Foreign Entity Report is September 2026. ABS covers 2024–25; RBA ends in March 2019. These are not a live market database: different dates and populations cannot form one directly comparable market snapshot. RBA coverage changes in 1980; the waterfall uses post-1980 endpoints and percentage-point changes, not returns.
